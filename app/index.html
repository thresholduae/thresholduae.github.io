About, terms and privacy

What this is. Threshold is a set of free tools for people renting or buying a home in the UAE. It gives general information to help you ask better questions. It cannot see the property, and it is not legal, financial, mortgage or investment advice.

The figures. Fees, deposits and percentages are typical published figures, last checked on 14 November 2026. They change, they differ between emirates, and your own contract may say something else. You can change every figure. Confirm the current ones with your agent, your bank, the Land Department or your utility provider before you commit. If you spot a wrong figure, tell us through the Consultant screen.

The decoder. It shows what listing phrases often mean. A highlighted phrase is a reason to ask a question, not proof of anything about a property, an agent or an owner.

Your decisions. You use the tools at your own risk and remain responsible for your own decisions. To the extent the law allows, the publisher accepts no liability for loss arising from reliance on the tools.

Privacy. What you type and save stays on this device and is never sent anywhere. The app adds one to an anonymous counter when it is opened and when a tool is opened; the counter records no text, no name and nothing you typed. If you send a consultation request, the details you entered are emailed to the consultant through a form delivery service, and are used only to reply to you, unless you ticked the box to receive tips.

Talking to a consultant. Consultations are free. A consultation is a conversation, not a regulated legal, mortgage or investment service. If you later buy or rent through the consultant, the usual agency commission for that transaction applies and is agreed in writing first.
