Off-plan from a developer, the developer pays the agent, so there's usually no agency fee, but the transfer fee still applies. Banks usually ask expatriate residents for at least 20% down on a first home under AED 5 million.

Running costs once you own it

  • Service charge: charged per square foot each year, and it varies a lot between buildings. Ask for the last two years' figures.
  • Housing fee: owners living in their home in Dubai pay 5% of its rental value, on the utility bill.
  • District cooling, utilities, insurance and upkeep: AC servicing, repairs and repainting add up. The calculator sets aside a share of the price each year.
