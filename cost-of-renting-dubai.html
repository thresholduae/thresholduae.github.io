The real cost of renting in Dubai

The rent in the ad is only part of it. Before you get the keys you'll usually pay the first cheque, two deposits, the agent and the set-up fees, and each month there's more on top of the rent.

The deposits are custom rather than law, so a landlord may ask for a different amount. Both should come back when you move out.

What you pay each month, on top of rent

  • Housing fee: 5% of the yearly rent, added to your DEWA bill in Dubai. On AED 85,000 that's about AED 354 a month.
  • Electricity and water: higher in summer, when the AC runs all day.
  • District cooling (chiller): only if the building isn't "chiller free". Ask before you sign, because it can be a large bill.
  • Internet, parking and getting around: Salik, fuel, metro or taxis.

Ways to pay less up front

  • Fewer cheques can get you a lower rent, but more cheques mean a smaller first payment. The calculator shows both.
  • Ask whether the home is chiller free and whether parking is included.
  • Renting directly from an owner can save the commission, but check ownership carefully. See the 9 scam checks.
