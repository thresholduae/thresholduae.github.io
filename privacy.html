Privacy policy

Threshold app · Last updated 19 November 2026

In short: Threshold has no adverts and does not track you. The ads you paste and everything you work out in the tools stay on your phone. Your details leave your phone only when you create the free account (first name and an email or WhatsApp number) or send a request to a consultant. We never sell them.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type or save in the tools stays in the app's storage on your device: ads you paste, saved results, calculator figures, checklists, comparisons and messages. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or using "Clear my data" in Settings, erases it.

The anonymous usage count

The app adds one to a public counter when it is opened, when each tool is opened, and when a request is sent. This tells us how many times the app and each tool are used. No text you type, no name, and no device or advertising ID is sent with it. The counter is run by a third-party service, Abacus. Like any internet service, it receives your connection's IP address when the count is made. We do not receive or store it.

When you send a request to a consultant

If you fill in the "Talk to a consultant" form and press Send, the app sends what you entered to our consultant by email: your name, phone number or email, what you need, area, budget, best time to contact you and your message. If you leave the box ticked, it also includes the summary of the last ad you decoded. The email is delivered by a third-party form service, FormSubmit.

We use these details only to reply to your request. If you tick "Send me occasional property tips", we may also contact you about property, and you can ask us to stop at any time. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

If you choose WhatsApp, email or phone instead, your message goes through that app, under its own privacy policy.

Your free account

The demo ads work without an account. To check your own ads, Threshold asks once for your first name and an email address or WhatsApp number. There is no password. We receive these details, whether you ticked "Send me the weekly Threshold brief", which link brought you to us (for example a newspaper article), and whether you signed up on the website or in the phone app. They reach us by email through FormSubmit or our own sign-up service, and email addresses are kept on our contact list with Resend, the service we use to send emails.

We use your details to keep your account, to tell you when the phone app is out, and to send the weekly brief only if you ticked that box. Every brief has a way to stop it. The ads you paste are not part of your account and are never sent to us. Your account is remembered on your device; "Clear my data" in Settings removes it from there.

Reading ads from a link in the phone app

When you paste a property link in the phone app, the app opens that page on your phone, as your browser would, and reads the ad's text there. The page and its text are not sent to us.

Websites opened from the app

If you open the consultant's website from the app, that website has its own privacy policy.

Children

Threshold is meant for adults renting or buying a home. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

To see or delete your account details, have a request you sent deleted, or stop the weekly brief, email us at the address below and we will do it.

Changes
